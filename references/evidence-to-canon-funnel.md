# Evidence-to-Canon Funnel

Use this funnel when exploration produces more information than the project's canonical product or engineering documents should carry.

The goal is not to preserve everything. The goal is to preserve **useful evidence without letting provisional material become current truth by accident**.

## Four layers

### 0. Transient

Conversation, scratch notes, raw ideas, and one-off observations.

Do not persist them merely because they exist. Persist only when they may matter beyond the current working session.

### 1. Evidence

Source-bound findings that may be useful later: research notes, user observations, benchmarks, competitor findings, experiments, and unresolved contradictions.

Evidence may be incomplete or provisional. Record enough source and context to re-evaluate it later.

### 2. Synthesis

A compact interpretation of relevant evidence: Option Map, comparison, problem framing, unknowns, or a focused research summary.

Synthesis reduces noise. It should not copy every evidence detail.

### 3. Canon

The small set of documents that describe what the project currently believes, intends, or promises: product thesis, approved scope, current specification, or operating contract.

Canon should contain **current truth, not the full history of how the team reached it**.

Use a Decision Record beside the funnel when a material, hard-to-reverse commitment needs a durable rationale or audit trail.

## Flow

```text
Transient
   ↓ worth preserving?
Evidence
   ↓ relevant and sufficiently supported?
Synthesis
   ↓ selected / committed?
Canon

Material hard-to-reverse choice ──→ Decision Record
```

Promotion is deliberate. Information should not move upward merely because it was written down.

## Promotion rules

Promote material only when it changes a decision, reduces an important uncertainty, or is stable enough to guide future work.

Before promotion, ask:

- Does this still matter to the current problem?
- Is it evidence, interpretation, or an actual commitment?
- Is material counter-evidence still unresolved?
- Can the higher-level document state the conclusion without copying the raw history?

Prefer links over duplication. Keep detailed evidence in its own note and let Canon contain the concise conclusion.

## Document restraint

Do not create a new document unless it has a distinct job or reader.

Avoid:

- dumping conversation transcripts into Canon;
- treating every research finding as a requirement;
- keeping multiple documents that claim to be the current truth;
- preserving superseded synthesis as if it were still active guidance;
- adding metadata, status systems, or folders that cost more attention than they save.

When a working note is superseded, mark it clearly or remove it when no longer useful. Git already preserves history.
